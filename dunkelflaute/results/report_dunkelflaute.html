How to read this

Trends inside a run are not climate change. 1950C and 2080C are fixed-forcing time slices: a trend across their winters is internal variability, and the p-values show that. The meaningful comparisons are between runs and against ERA5.

Caveats. Solar is a reconstruction (model v5, exact monthly rescale) whose skill is validated on ERA5 and SMARD only; no ground truth exists for DART. The 2080C result assumes today's cloud-to-irradiance physics holds. Wind uses a 100 m log-law reconstruction. 1952 Feb/Mar solar inputs are missing from the archive but fall in the spin-up-excluded winter. Winters used: 1953–1969 (1950C), 2083–2092 (2080C), spin-up years excluded.
