Revised after a methodology check against the actual paper. The first pass of this analysis had two mismatches with Mockert et al.'s Section 2.4, both now fixed: (1) it windowed on Oct–Feb only, dropping September even though the paper explicitly counts the whole year and finds the bulk of events fall in autumn (Sep–Nov) and winter (Dec–Feb) — Sep–Mar is what this pipeline's solar reconstruction actually has data for, so that's the window used now; and (2) event duration was measured as simple threshold-crossing runs of the rolling mean, but the paper's actual construction is different: "All time steps contributing to a running mean below the threshold are considered part of the Dunkelflaute. Therefore, the minimum duration of a Dunkelflaute is 48h [...] by definition." That means a single below-threshold rolling-mean sample pulls its whole 48-hour contributing window into the event, not just that one instant — now implemented that way below, which is why every event here is ≥2 days.
